# Il·lustracions de la lectura L094

Imatges generades amb el generador integrat i optimitzades per al catàleg.

### l094-pez-en-la-plancha.webp

```text
Original landscape 3:2 educational book illustration in a handmade relief-print style, warm cream paper, rough linocut textures, deep pine green and muted persimmon ink, restrained blocks of color. View directly down at a classroom stamp-making table. One small rectangle of soft light-colored craft foam sits horizontally. On the foam there is one simple lightly indented line drawing of a fish with its head and eye on the RIGHT and tail on the LEFT; near the top edge of the plate is a single little crescent moon. A blunt rounded stylus lies beside it, plus one small hand roller with a clearly visible cylinder. The plate is still clean and pale, before inking. Composed tactile scene with slightly irregular hand-drawn lines, no knives, fingers, text, captions, labels, multiple fish, ink spills, or diagrams.
```

### l094-pez-estampado.webp

```text
Make a new horizontal 3:2 companion illustration based on the exact foam-plate drawing in the previous image. The focus is a single finished print on a cream rectangular sheet resting slightly diagonally on a worn persimmon worktable. The inked rectangular area is deep pine green with subtle blockprint grain. The fish is drawn only in thin cream incised lines within the ink field (the body stays green). It is an exact HORIZONTAL MIRROR of the previous fish: the eye, short conical head and gill are on the LEFT, tail clearly on the RIGHT. The little crescent remains ABOVE the fish, now above-left of center and opening toward the upper-left, the horizontal mirror of the original moon. The 2D sheet lies flat; show its unobtrusive paper-matte texture. At the edge, part of a roller or a few clean paper offcuts can be cropped. One fish and one moon only, no other specimens, people, words, captions, symbols, knives, or hands. Same handmade pine green, persimmon and cream printmaking aesthetic.
```
