# Il·lustracions de la lectura L095

Imatges generades amb el generador integrat i optimitzades per al catàleg.

### l095-lava-al-mar.webp

```text
A horizontal 3:2 science editorial illustration for Spanish 6th grade, original visual interpretation of lava from the 2018 Kīlauea eruption meeting ocean water on the Island of Hawaiʻi. Expressive charcoal and compressed conté drawing on cool blue-gray toned paper, atmospheric graphic strata, black lava rock and low shelves of solidified basalt, chalk-white surf. A secondary narrow ribbon of incandescent orange flows down from a low dark coastal lava bank into the ocean and makes one drifting pale steam plume immediately at the point of contact. Keep the contact site small in the broad coastal view. Show a coastline with surf, sensible gravity and distances, no tall volcano cone near the sea, no human figures, buildings, captions, numbers, collage panels, or fantasy flames. Palette nearly monochrome except for the lava; visible charcoal grain and a strong horizon.
```

### l095-arena-en-la-orilla.webp

```text
Companion 3:2 scientific illustration to the previous cool blue-gray charcoal and conté landscape of a Hawaiian lava coast. New perspective: a quiet close low overhead view of the edge of a formed BLACK SAND beach, with a thin white breaking wave washing diagonally from upper right to lower left. Foreground gentle slope of fine charcoal-black sand with a handful of small dark rounded volcanic pebbles, very visible grains and subtle water channels. Sea is slate gray-blue, stylized soft ripples, chalk-white foam, no sky required. Emphasize the visual texture difference between loose grains and water, natural and unforced. No red or orange in this view, no active lava, no huge boulders, people, footprints, signs, letters, diagrams, or words. Hand-drawn charcoal texture and luminous clear values on blue-toned paper.
```
