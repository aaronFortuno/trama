# Il·lustracions de la lectura L090

Imatges generades amb el generador integrat i optimitzades per al catàleg.

### l090-sobre-asirio-cerrado.webp

```text
Landscape 3:2 museum-education illustration for an original Spanish sixth-grade reading about an ancient Old Assyrian clay letter. Refined drypoint print and watercolor with a warm sandstone and burnt umber palette, tiny ink hatching, cream paper, subtle shadow. Art reconstructs a single intact sealed clay envelope from the early second millennium BCE in Anatolia, an irregular palm-sized flat rounded square slab about 5 cm across and about 2 cm thick, subdued terracotta, not a paper envelope and with no modern triangular flap. On top, a horizontal rectangular band of shallow ancient seal impressions and a few short wedge incisions. Show the sealed piece at a three-quarter angle on a plain warm museum display mat, isolated, with the scale suggested by a simple nearby slender reed stylus. Rich tactility and careful shape, plenty of negative space; no human figures, no modern lettering, no label, no decorative border.
```

### l090-tablilla-y-fragmentos.webp

```text
Companion museum-education illustration in the previous museum plate's precise drypoint hatching and warm sandstone watercolor on cream paper, horizontal 3:2. Top-down display with two separate components of a reconstructed ancient Old Assyrian clay letter: on the left, one little flat square rounded terracotta tablet with many rows of small wedge-shaped indentations pressed into its surface, intact, and on the right, a few curved fragments of a coarser clay envelope. Each shell fragment is thin (several millimeters), and one is turned hollow-side up so one sees the shallow concave inner wall; the tablet could fit within the full envelope, and none of the fragments cover the tablet. Avoid modern symbols, Arabic or Latin lettering, captions, frames, diagrams, or people. Elegant moderate close-up, authentic archaeological texture.
```
